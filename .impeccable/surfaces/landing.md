# Surface: Landing (`/`)

Seed key: `a34dab65` · Mode: **Persuade** · World: **The Transit Supplement** · Build: code-led

## Scope and mode

Replace the visual world of the landing page only. The `<GridDistortion>` hero block is
**fixed by owner instruction** and ships byte-for-byte. Product truth, features, routes and
function are preserved. The `/search` and `/result` surfaces are out of scope and inherit the
incumbent daisyUI treatment until their own run.

## Audience, job, action

Primary user is a couple or group organiser (PRODUCT.md). They arrive skeptical of AI trip
planners and must believe that a day is a *route*, not a list. Then they must act: start a
plan. No proof assets exist, so nothing on this page may imply usage, approval or results.

The action is the three inputs the product actually consumes, in the form's own vocabulary:
a ruled slip set into the supplement's columns.

## Constraints

- Hero untouched.
- No testimonial, rating, user-count or press content of any kind.
- No emoji as iconography. No gradient text. No kicker/eyebrow above a heading.
- Copied factual claims that the product can back (feature list, flow steps) may be
  re-voiced; nothing factual may be invented.
- `picsum.photos` placeholder imagery is replaced where it stood in for a destination.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

**The Transit Supplement.** A trip is a service that issued a printed route insert. The
category default stacks a photo hero over a card grid of benefits; this refuses to summarise
the trip at all and shows the routing device the product actually produces.

**Memorable moment:** the seam. The fixed grayscale photograph ends on a hard, slightly
irregular cut against the paper supplement, and the *first* thing below it is a live route —
contour bands with a single prussian line threading them from a marked origin to a marked
terminus, the line drawn with the keyboard-accessible fields the visitor can change. The
visitor sees the product's thesis before they read a word of it.

## Verdict and raises carried from the roll

Assigned direction beat every challenger on both axes; each donation is a raised line on the
build:

- **Direction of travel** (Versailles) — the route reads as movement along an axis, origin to
  terminus, never a table row.
- **One fold, one silhouette** (paper folds) — photograph meets paper across a single seam.
- **Single-ink discipline; dated numbered run** (polar ice press).
- **Exactly one saturated ink** (civic bureau) — spent only on the route line and primary action.
- **Sacred column lattice** (teletext) — scales rather than reflows; every colour means one thing.
